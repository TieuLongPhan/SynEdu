Talktorials
===========

SynEdu talktorials are executable Jupyter notebooks rendered with
``nbsphinx`` + ``nbsphinx-link``.
They are organized both as **curated collections** and as a **complete, flat list**.

Use the collections to follow a learning path, or jump directly to any individual
talktorial below.


Collections
-----------

The collections group talktorials by *what you build*.
They are designed to be followed sequentially, but each notebook remains independent.
